# Story and sound credits

- Story extension, page art and score: Pakson family reading project.
- Original synthetic voice references and speech: [Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS), Apache-2.0 models. VoiceDesign descriptions create the references; Base uses those references and does not apply per-block emotion instructions. No model weights are served.
- Original Pika character calls are generated directly with Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign descriptions. They are not Base dialogue clips or official actor recordings.
- The separate short /d/ teaching cue: [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), Apache-2.0 model, raw consonant generation at normal speed.
- [Cartoon - Duck Quack by Breviceps](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/445960/), CC0 1.0, from the author’s public HQ preview.
- Pikachu calls are original AI character interpretations, not official voice-actor recordings. The child role is an original synthetic voice, not a clone of a child recording.
- Music and the remaining Foley are unchanged original project assets. The printed song lines use rhythmic spoken delivery; no original melody is claimed.
- Original child photographs, private character references, model weights, credentials and full project backups are excluded from the published website.
- Paper curl rendering: [StPageFlip](https://github.com/Nodlik/StPageFlip), version 2.0.7, MIT licence; the unmodified browser library and its licence are bundled locally.
